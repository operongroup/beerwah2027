Beerwah@Night 2027

The Vlog Brief

How to film a 27-week build without it becoming a second job.

This is the standing brief. It does not change week to week. What changes is the weekly content pack, which lives in the Content tab of your training app and gives you that week's shot list, talking points and change log.

The one rule

Film first, decide later. You are not making a video each week. You are collecting footage that becomes something later. Ninety seconds of filming per session is the whole commitment. If a session is hard, film less. The training always wins.

01The story you are actually telling

Not "man trains for race." That is every running channel. Yours has a sharper spine, and it showed up properly in weeks five and six:

A 37-year-old builder with three young kids, running at 4:30am, tries to take fourteen minutes off a night trail race — and keeps discovering his own training plan is built on things nobody checked.

The course was measured off the official KML and the decisive climb turned out to be in the wrong place. The hill five minutes from his door turned out to be a quarter of the length needed. The trail he ran easy with his dad turned out to be the race climb. And he opened a session card in the app, asked why a time trial was prescribing hill sprints, and uncovered eighty-six mistakes.

That tension — doing the work while the plan keeps being wrong — is the thing that separates this from a training diary. Lean into it. The corrections are the content.

03The weekly rhythm

MON
Ninety seconds to camera

What this week is meant to do, and what you are worried about. Filmed at the desk, before anything has happened.

TUE
The quality session

One shot before, one during if it is safe, one after while you are still breathing hard. That is the whole thing.

WED/THU
Nothing, usually

Easy runs are not content. Skip them unless something happens: a lizard, a storm, a sunrise worth the phone coming out.

FRI
One strength clip

A single set, filmed side on. Ten seconds. It exists to show the unglamorous half.

WEEKEND
The graveyard

Your best footage every single week. Real climbing, real descents, real effort. Film the climb from the bottom, and film yourself at the top not talking.

SUN PM
The wrap

Two or three minutes at the desk with the app open. What the data said, what changed, what you got wrong.

04Six shots that carry any episode

  1. The 4:30am open. Dark street, torch on, nothing said. Establishes the cost of this in one frame.
  2. The prescription on screen. Screen-record the session card. It shows there is a real plan, not vibes.
  3. Effort, unedited. Breathing hard, hands on knees, no commentary. Resist the urge to talk over it.
  4. A number that surprised you. Point at the data. "That's four minutes thirty-three per kilometre and I didn't know I could do that."
  5. The correction. Every time the plan gets fixed, that is a scene. This is your differentiator.
  6. Something real from home. The one-year-old up at 1am. The 30-minute drive you could not make. This is why people watch.

05Talking to camera without cringing

  • Say the number. "I ran 4:44 per kilometre for three minutes" beats "it felt pretty good."
  • Say what you got wrong. You already do this in conversation. Do it on camera.
  • One idea per clip. If you need two, film two clips.
  • Do not re-record. The stumble is the texture. Nobody re-records their thoughts in real life.
  • End on the question, not the answer. "The time trial is in two weeks and I have no idea what it'll say."

06The arc, so you know what you are building toward

W1-8
Base, and finding out the plan is wrong

Flu, travel, a hill that was too short, a course measured properly for the first time. Ends with the week 8 time trial on 15 September and the first honest verdict.

W9-16
Build

The graveyard becomes the centre of the week. Volume climbs toward 50km. Second time trial 10 November: the 21:30 gate.

W17-22
Specific, and it gets dark

Night trail sessions with the head torch. The Beerwah@Daybreak tune-up race on 6 December, same course in daylight, first real time on the ground.

W23-27
Taper and the race

Full dress rehearsal at Beerwah at night on 9 January. Then 30 January, 7:00pm, the one it was all for.
